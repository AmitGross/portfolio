Background & Timeline

Amit Gross is an interdisciplinary STEM researcher working across life sciences, data, and scientific operations. His background spans neurobiology, wet-lab biology and behavioral research, bioinformatics, connectomics, Python-based analysis, and support for scientific infrastructure.

His work often sits between research questions and practical implementation: defining biological or operational systems, organizing their components, attributes, and interactions, and turning them into workflows, analyses, figures, or processes that others can use. This perspective connects his full-brain (connectome) research at the University of Haifa, imaging and core-facility support at the Weizmann Institute of Science, data and information-systems work connected to IQVIA / Moderna EMEA, and teaching in Python, research methods, neurohistology, microscopy, and scientific workflows.

This portfolio reflects that mixed profile: scientific research, computational thinking, operational execution, communication, and creative problem solving.
