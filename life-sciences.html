Research & Neurobiology

This section focuses on my biological and experimental background.
My life sciences experience includes neurobiology research using behavioral experiments, rodent models, DNA-related methods, and molecular work. At the Weizmann Institute, I developed strong expertise in optical imaging and immunostaining while consulting, supporting, and training students and scientists across many biological labs and broader STEM projects.
